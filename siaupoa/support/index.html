Report an AI Issue

If a recognition result is incorrect, inappropriate, or offensive, please let us know. You can report it directly in the app by going to  — Settings → About & Support → Report an AI Issue — or email support@goodsvalu.com directly.

To help us investigate, please include:

  • What you submitted — for example, a receipt photo or text you typed or dictated.
  • What the app returned — and what you expected the result to be.
  • Your app version and phone model — you can find the app version at the bottom of Settings.

Please don’t include any personal or sensitive information you’re not comfortable sharing. A brief description of the issue is usually enough.

Delete your account

You can delete your account directly in the app at any time—no need to contact us. If you no longer have the app installed, you can contact us for help with deleting your account.

From inside the App

Settings → About & Support → Delete Account.

By email, if you cannot reach the App

Email support@goodsvalu.com with the subject "Delete my account", from the email address associated with your account — that address is how we confirm the request is really yours. We complete the deletion within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account
  • Your AI usage records
  • Your subscription status and entitlement records stored on our servers

What is kept, and why

Limited information retained after deletion

When you delete your account, almost all account data is deleted. We retain only limited information needed to manage free AI recognition allowances and prevent the free allowance from being reset by deleting and re-creating an account.

How the free allowance works

New users receive 10 free AI recognitions during their first calendar month. After that, the free allowance resets to 3 AI recognitions per calendar month. Deleting and re-creating an account does not start a new first-month allowance. If the new account is associated with an email address or device that has already been used, the remaining free allowance for that calendar month is carried over, with a maximum of 3 free recognitions. At the beginning of a new calendar month, the allowance resets to 3 free recognitions. The introductory 10-recognition allowance is available only to genuinely new users and is not granted again after an account is deleted and re-created.

What we retain

To apply these rules, we retain limited records that allow us to determine:

  • whether an email address or device has already received the first-month allowance; and
  • the AI recognition usage associated with that email address or device for the current calendar month.

We do not retain your actual email address or device identifier for this purpose. Instead, the identifiers are stored as one-way salted hashes and are not stored in their original form.

Current-month usage information is no longer needed after the relevant calendar month ends. Records needed to determine whether the introductory allowance has already been received may be retained to prevent the introductory allowance from being claimed repeatedly.

This is a limited exception to the account deletion described above. For more information, see Section 10 of our Privacy Policy.

What is NOT deleted

Your bookkeeping data stays on your device.

 Your ledgers, accounts, categories, and transactions are stored only on your device and are not stored on our servers. Deleting your account therefore does not delete this data.

To delete this data, go to Settings → Data → Reset. You can also remove it by uninstalling the app.

Your paid subscription.
Your subscription is managed through your Google Play account. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription.

To avoid future charges, cancel your subscription separately in the Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Stored only on your device.
Your ledgers, accounts, categories, and transactions are stored in a local database on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. For more information about how your data is handled, see our Privacy Policy

How do I move to a new phone?

Moving your data to a new phone

On your old phone, go to Settings → Data → Backup and save the backup file somewhere you can access from your new phone.

On your new phone, install the app, then go to Settings → Data → Restore and select your backup file.

Do I need an account?

Only for AI recognition.
You only need an account to use AI recognition. Sign in with your email address and the one-time code we send you—there’s no password or separate sign-up process.

Everything else, including manual entry, reports, budgets, recurring transactions, and backup/restore, works without an account.

Your account is used to keep track of your monthly AI recognition allowance, so your remaining allowance stays with you if you switch phones or reinstall the app.

I didn't get my sign-in code

Didn't receive your sign-in code?
First, check your spam or junk folder. It may take a minute for the code to arrive. If you still don’t see it, tap “Send a new code” on the sign-in screen.

Sign-in codes expire, so be sure to use the most recent code you received.

Can I use the App without the AI feature?

Yes. The app works without AI recognition.
Manual entry, reports, budgets, recurring transactions, backup, and restore all work entirely on your device, without sending your bookkeeping data to our servers.

If you decline or withdraw consent for AI recognition, only photo and voice recognition will be unavailable. All other features will continue to work normally.

How do I withdraw my AI consent?

You can withdraw your consent at any time by going to Settings → Privacy → AI Recognition & Data Processing → Withdraw Consent.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete your account above for instructions, including how to delete your account if you no longer have the app installed.

Why does AI recognition say I have run out?

New users receive 10 free AI recognitions during their first calendar month. After that, you receive 3 free AI recognitions each calendar month. Your current usage and remaining allowance are shown in the App under Settings → Account. The allowance resets at the start of each calendar month (UTC). Manual entry is always available and does not count toward your AI recognition allowance.

Is a photo of my receipt kept anywhere?

No. Your receipt photo is not stored after recognition. It is used only while the recognition is being processed and is automatically deleted from your device as soon as the process finishes, whether the recognition succeeds or fails. The photo is not saved to permanent storage or displayed anywhere in the App, and our servers do not retain a copy.
