Privacy Policy

Siaupoa · Last updated: 14 September 2026

In one sentence: Your bookkeeping data stays on your device. The only bookkeeping content sent to our servers is a receipt photo or text that you explicitly submit for AI recognition, and only after you have given your consent.

1. Who we are

Siaupoa ("the App") is developed and operated by Goodsvalu Inc. ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, contact support@goodsvalu.com.

2. What stays on your device

Your bookkeeping data is stored only on your device. The following information is kept in a local database on your phone and is never uploaded to our servers:

  • Ledgers, accounts, categories and budgets
  • Every transaction — amount, date, merchant, notes, currency
  • Your app settings, including your display language and currency
  • Reminder schedules — Reminders are scheduled entirely on your device. The App does not use push notifications or register your device for push notifications.

Backups are created only when you request them. You choose where to save or share the backup file using your device’s share menu, such as your personal cloud storage, email, or local storage. We do not receive or store your backup files.

3. What Data Leaves Your Device, and When

Your bookkeeping data stays on your device unless you explicitly use a feature that requires data to be sent. There are only three cases:

When you take or select a receipt photo and confirm itThe receipt image, resized and converted to JPEG on your device before being sentOur server, then securely forwarded to our AI provider
When you type or dictate a description and tap SubmitThat textOur server, then our AI provider for processing

The first two occur only after you have given consent through the in-app consent screen, and only when you submit an AI recognition request. Along with the receipt photo or text you submit, the request includes the names of your expense categories so the AI can select a category that already exists in your books. It also includes the current date, your app version, and a randomly generated one-time request identifier.

The third case — checking your subscription status — is not covered by the AI consent screen because it does not involve your bookkeeping content. No receipt photo, recognition text, transaction data, or email address is sent for this purpose.

The App sends only an identifier: your account identifier if you are signed in, or an installation identifier generated by our subscription provider if you are not. This identifier is used solely to determine whether you have an active subscription.

The App contacts the subscription provider only when it needs to check your subscription status. Simply opening the App, entering transactions, or viewing your books does not send your bookkeeping data to us or to the subscription provider.

Photos

  • Receipt photos are never saved to permanent storage or displayed anywhere in the App.
  • Before a receipt image is sent, it is resized and re-encoded on your device, and its metadata — including EXIF, XMP, IPTC, and comments — is explicitly removed. This metadata can contain information such as GPS coordinates, so removing it ensures that location data stored in the image is not transmitted. As an additional safeguard, our server checks every received image for metadata and alerts us if any metadata is detected.
  • As soon as recognition is complete — whether it succeeds or fails — the temporary image file is automatically deleted from your device.

Voice

The App does not include its own speech-recognition feature and does not request microphone access for this purpose. When you dictate an entry, you are using your phone keyboard’s built-in dictation feature, which is provided by your operating system. The App receives only the resulting text. No audio is ever recorded, accessed, or transmitted by the App.

4. What our server stores

AI recognition requests pass through our server before reaching the AI provider so we can track usage and manage service costs. Our server does not store the content you submit. Its database has no fields designed to store receipt images or the text you provide. This protection is built into the system's architecture, rather than relying solely on a promise not to retain your content.

5. What we never collect

  • We do not collect your name or phone number.
  • No password — sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your email, so the App never needs to collect or store a password.
  • No access to your contacts, calendar, location, or photo library

    • No advertising identifiers — there is no advertising in the App. The App uses a device identifier provided by your operating system (Apple’s Identifier for Vendor, or Android’s SSAID) only to prevent the free AI recognition allowance from being claimed repeatedly. It is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites, and it is never sent to our AI provider. Our server converts the identifier into a one-way hash and stores only the hash, not the original identifier. Separately, our subscription provider generates its own identifier for the App installation. This is used to associate the installation with the correct subscription. It is kept separate from the hashed device identifier described above and is not used by us for advertising or cross-app or cross-website tracking.

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are included in the App. The only third-party SDK we use is provided by our subscription provider. Purchase and renewal events are processed through that SDK and may appear in the provider’s dashboard. See Section 7 for more information.
  • No financial account credentials — the App does not connect to your bank or other financial accounts.

6. Your account

An account is required only for AI recognition. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code sent to your email — no password is required or stored. All other features in the App work without an account.

The account is used to keep your monthly free AI recognition allowance consistent across reinstalls and device changes. Without an account, reinstalling the App could incorrectly grant a new free allowance. Your email address is used only for account access and is not used for marketing. We send you only the sign-in codes needed to access your account. A session credential is stored using your device’s secure credential storage.

8. International transfer

We are established in the United States. If you are located outside the United States, when you submit a receipt photo or text for AI recognition, that content is transferred to the United States for processing by Anthropic PBC. Before the first such transfer, the App informs you of this transfer through the AI consent screen and asks for your consent. The transfer occurs only when you choose to use AI recognition and submit content for processing.

The usage records described in Section 4 are stored on servers located in Japan and may be accessed by us from the United States.

9. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

  • Consent — for sending receipt photos or text to our AI provider for AI recognition. You provide consent through the in-app consent screen and may withdraw it at any time under Settings → Privacy. Withdrawing consent disables AI recognition but does not affect any other features of the App.
  • Legitimate interests — for maintaining the usage records described in Section 4 to enforce the free AI recognition allowance, prevent and detect abuse, and manage the operational costs of providing the AI recognition service.

10. Retention

  • Photos and text: Not retained by us. They are processed only in memory for the duration of the AI recognition request and are discarded when processing is complete.
  • Per-request usage records: Retained for as long as your account remains active.
  • Allowance-related hashes and current-month usage count: Retained even after you delete your account. These limited records are retained to prevent the free AI recognition allowance from being reset or claimed again by deleting and re-creating an account. We do not retain your original email address or device identifier for this purpose; instead, we retain one-way, salted hashes of those identifiers. The usage count contains only the number of AI recognitions used during the current calendar month. These records are used only to enforce the free AI recognition allowance. The current-month usage count is deleted when the relevant calendar month ends.
  • Your subscription record: Retained for as long as your account exists and deleted when you delete your account. Expired subscription records are retained until account deletion so that we can respond to questions about your subscription history.
  • Your bookkeeping data: Stored only on your device and retained until you delete it. We cannot access, retrieve, or delete this data on your behalf.

11. Your rights and controls

  • Withdraw AI consent: Go to Settings → Privacy → AI Recognition & Data Processing to withdraw your consent at any time. After you withdraw consent, no receipt photos or text will be sent for AI recognition. All other features of the App will continue to work normally.

  • Delete your account: Go to Settings → About & Support → Delete Account. Deleting your account permanently deletes your account, email address, and the per-request usage records described in Section 4. Limited allowance-related records are retained after account deletion: the one-way, salted hashes described in Section 10 and your AI recognition usage count for the current calendar month. These records are retained only to prevent the free AI recognition allowance from being reset or claimed again by deleting and re-creating an account. The current-month usage count is deleted when the relevant calendar month ends. See Section 10 for more information.

    Deleting your account does not delete the bookkeeping data stored on your device, because we do not have access to that data. It also does not cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions are managed through your Google Play or Apple account and must be cancelled through the applicable app store.

  • Delete your local data: Go to Settings → Data → Reset to delete the bookkeeping data stored on your device. Uninstalling the App will also remove its locally stored data.
  • Export your data: Go to Settings → Data → Backup to create a complete copy of your bookkeeping database. Transaction lists can also be exported in CSV, Excel, or PDF format.
  • Access, correction, and objection: Because your bookkeeping data is stored only on your device, these rights generally apply to the account and usage information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@goodsvalu.com.

12. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, or under the applicable minimum age of digital consent in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the applicable age. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without the required consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data in a way that materially affects you, we will update this Privacy Policy. If a change affects how your data is processed for AI recognition and requires new consent, we will ask for your consent again within the App rather than relying on consent you previously provided.
