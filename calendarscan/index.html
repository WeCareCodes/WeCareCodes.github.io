Features

Every date from the paper on the fridge, in your calendar

Any paper, PDF or message

Scan printed calendars and notices, import a PDF or photo, paste text from an email, or share straight from another app.

You check before anything is added

The AI highlights anything it isn’t sure about, next to the original text, so you can fix it in seconds.

Made for families

Pick whose calendar it is to use that child’s color and put their name in front of each title. Scan an updated calendar and add only what changed.

Private by design

Photos and text are sent only when you scan, used to read the dates, and not stored on our server. Your calendars are never uploaded. No ads, no tracking.

How it works

From paper to calendar in 3 steps

  1. 1

    Snap

    Photograph the calendar or notice, or import a PDF.

  2. 2

    Review

    Check the dates the AI found; anything uncertain is highlighted.

  3. 3

    Add

    Choose a calendar and add everything at once. Undo anytime from History.
