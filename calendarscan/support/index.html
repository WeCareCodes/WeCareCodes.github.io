Common questions

What can CalendarScan read?

Paper or PDF calendars, school notices, flyers, invitations, appointment cards, class schedules, and text from emails or messages. Deadlines such as “return the form by 10/15” are added to Reminders.

Why are some items highlighted in yellow?

The AI was not sure about them — for example, blurry text or a date that looked wrong. Tap an item to see the original text and fix it before adding.

How do I undo an import?

On the Done screen tap Undo, or open Import history on the home screen, choose the import and tap Undo. A calendar or list created by that import is also deleted if it is empty.

The school sent a revised calendar.

Scan it again. CalendarScan compares it with your last import and can add only what changed, removing the old versions of moved events.

How do I share a calendar with my family?

Add events to an iCloud calendar, then tap “Share this calendar” on the Done screen for step-by-step instructions, or export an .ics file from Import history.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for what is deleted.
