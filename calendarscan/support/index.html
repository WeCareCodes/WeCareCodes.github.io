Support

CalendarScan

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@goodsvalu.com or use Settings → Feedback in the App (it includes your App version and your iOS or Android version). We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

What can CalendarScan read?

Paper or PDF calendars, school notices, flyers, invitations, appointment cards, class schedules, and text from emails or messages. Deadlines such as “return the form by 10/15” are added to Reminders on iPhone. On Android they are added to your calendar marked ☐, with an alert the day before.

Why are some items highlighted in yellow?

The AI was not sure about them — for example, blurry text or a date that looked wrong. Tap an item to see the original text and fix it before adding.

How do I undo an import?

On the Done screen tap Undo, or open the History tab, choose the import and tap Undo. A calendar or list created by that import is also deleted if it is empty.

The school sent a revised calendar.

Scan it again. CalendarScan compares it with your last import and can add only what changed, removing the old versions of moved events.

How do I share a calendar with my family?

On iPhone, add events to an iCloud calendar. On Android, add them to a calendar in your Google account (calendars the App creates stay on that phone only). Then tap “Share this calendar” on the Done screen for step-by-step instructions, or export an .ics file from the History tab.

Delete your account

In the App: Settings → Delete Account. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the App: email support@goodsvalu.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my CalendarScan account”. We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your sign-in account and email address, recognition records, page-credit balance and purchase records linked to your account. Unused page credits cannot be restored after deletion.

What is kept: monthly usage counts linked to one-way hashes (no content), so the free allowance cannot be reset by re-registering, and records of which store purchases have already been credited, so one purchase is never credited twice. Events already added to your calendar and the App’s history on your phone are not affected — delete them on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
