Privacy Policy

CalendarScan · Last updated: 29 September 2026

In short: photos, files and text you choose to scan are sent to our server only to find dates and events, are passed to our AI provider for that purpose, and are not stored by us. The events you confirm are written to your iPhone Calendar and Reminders — we never upload your calendars. There are no ads and no tracking.

1. Who we are

CalendarScan (“the App”) is developed and operated by Goodsvalu Inc. (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question, contact support@goodsvalu.com.

2. What stays on your iPhone

  • The events and reminders you add, which the App writes to the iPhone Calendar and Reminders apps. The App reads your list of calendars and reminder lists, and events in the relevant dates, only to let you choose where to add, to warn about duplicates, and to undo an import.
  • Your import history (so you can export, undo, re-add or compare a revised calendar), an unfinished review, the names and colors of children you add, and your settings.
  • Items you share into the App from other apps, until you recognize or discard them.
  • If you turn it on, a copy of pages you scan with the camera is saved to your Photos library. The App asks only for permission to add photos, not to read them.

Deleting the App deletes this information from the App. Events already added to your Calendar and Reminders stay there until you delete them.

4. What our server stores

Our server does not store the images, files or text you submit, or the events the AI finds. It stores:

  • Your email address and account identifier (Supabase Auth).
  • For each recognized page: time, status, AI model, token usage, cost, response time and number of events found — to track costs and reliability.
  • Monthly usage counts linked to one-way hashes of your email, account and device identifiers — to enforce the free allowance. These contain no content and are kept after you delete your account, so the allowance cannot be reset by re-registering.
  • Your page-credit balance and purchase records, once purchases are available.
  • The review numbers described above.

6. Your choices

  • Delete your account anytime in the App: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in account, recognition records and credits. It does not delete events in your Calendar or the App’s history on your phone.
  • Turn off saving scans to Photos in Settings, and manage Calendar, Reminders, Camera and Photos permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Email support@goodsvalu.com to ask what we hold about you or to request deletion.

7. Children

CalendarScan is intended for parents and other adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the App.
