Privacy Policy

CalendarScan · Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short: photos, files and text you choose to scan are sent to our server only to find dates and events, are passed to our AI provider for that purpose, and are not stored by us. The events you confirm are written to the calendar on your phone (and, on iPhone, to Reminders) — we never upload your calendars. There are no ads and no tracking.

1. Who we are

CalendarScan (“the App”), for iPhone and Android, is developed and operated by Goodsvalu Inc. (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question, contact calendarscan@goodsvalu.com.

2. What stays on your phone

  • The events and reminders you add. On iPhone, the App writes them to the Calendar and Reminders apps. On Android, it writes them to the phone’s calendar (including calendars in your Google account, if you choose one); deadlines are added as calendar events marked ☐. The App reads your list of calendars and reminder lists, and events in the relevant dates, only to let you choose where to add, to warn about duplicates, and to undo an import.
  • Your import history (so you can export, undo, re-add or compare a revised calendar), an unfinished review, the names and colors of children you add, and your settings.
  • Items you share into the App from other apps, until you recognize or discard them.
  • If you turn it on, a copy of pages you scan with the camera is saved to your photos (on iPhone, the Photos library; on Android, the Pictures/CalendarScan folder). The App never reads your photo library: on iPhone it asks only for permission to add photos, and on Android it needs no photo permission. Photos you pick to scan are chosen in the system photo picker, which shares only the photos you select.

Deleting the App deletes this information from the App. Events already added to your calendar (and Reminders) stay there until you delete them.

4. What our server stores

Our server does not store the images, files or text you submit, or the events the AI finds. It stores:

  • Your email address and account identifier (Supabase Auth).
  • For each recognized page: time, status, AI model, token usage, cost, response time and number of events found — to track costs and reliability.
  • Monthly usage counts linked to one-way hashes of your email, account and device identifiers — to enforce the free allowance. These contain no content and are kept after you delete your account, so the allowance cannot be reset by re-registering.
  • Your page-credit balance and purchase records.
  • The store transaction IDs of purchases that have already been credited, without your account or email — so one purchase is never credited twice. These are kept after you delete your account.
  • The review numbers described above.

6. Your choices

  • Delete your account anytime in the App: Settings → Delete Account. This deletes your sign-in account, recognition records, credits and purchase records, including your purchase history at RevenueCat. We keep only the store transaction IDs described in section 4, without your account or email. Apple and Google keep their own order records under their policies. It does not delete events in your calendar or the App’s history on your phone. If you no longer have the App, see how to request deletion by email.
  • On iPhone, withdraw your permission to send content to Anthropic in the App’s Settings → AI Scanning. You will be asked again before your next recognition.
  • Turn off saving scans to photos in the App’s Settings. Manage permissions in iOS Settings → CalendarScan, or on Android in Settings → Apps → CalendarScan → Permissions.
  • Email calendarscan@goodsvalu.com to ask what we hold about you or to request deletion.

7. Children

CalendarScan is intended for parents and other adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the App.
