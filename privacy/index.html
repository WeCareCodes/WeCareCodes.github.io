隱私權政策

公司網站隱私權政策

我們如何在 goodsvalu.com 蒐集、使用與保護你的資料。

適用範圍

本政策說明 Goodsvalu Inc.（以下稱「我們」）在公司網站 goodsvalu.com 的首頁、產品、開發服務、關於我們與聯絡我們等頁面，如何蒐集與使用資料。各 App（CalendarScan、ValetGuy、Siaupoa、PassNest）有各自的隱私權政策，不適用本政策。

我們蒐集的資料

  • 你主動提供的資料：透過詢價表單或 Email 提供的姓名、Email、公司名稱與專案內容。
  • 瀏覽資料：透過 Google Analytics 與 Google Ads 的 cookie 或類似技術，蒐集瀏覽的頁面、來源（例如點了哪則廣告）、裝置與瀏覽器類型、概略地區等資料。

使用目的

  • 回覆你的詢價、提供報價與後續聯繫。
  • 了解網站使用狀況，並衡量廣告成效（例如有多少人透過廣告送出詢價）。

我們不會出售你的個人資料。

第三方服務

  • Google Analytics 與 Google Ads（Google LLC）：網站分析與廣告轉換追蹤。可參考 Google 如何使用這些資料。
  • Formspree：詢價表單的傳送服務，表單內容會經由 Formspree 轉寄到我們的信箱。

你的選擇

  • 你可以在 Google 廣告設定 關閉個人化廣告。
  • 你可以安裝 Google Analytics 停用外掛程式，或在瀏覽器中封鎖、刪除 cookie。
  • 你可以來信要求查閱、更正或刪除你提供給我們的資料。

資料保存

詢價資料保存到合作結束或你要求刪除為止；分析資料依 Google Analytics 的保存設定（最長 14 個月）。

聯絡我們與更新

對本政策有任何問題，請來信 support@goodsvalu.com。本政策如有修改，會更新於此頁。

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 11 日

Privacy policy

Website Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information on goodsvalu.com.

Scope

This policy explains how Goodsvalu Inc. (“we”) collects and uses data on the company website goodsvalu.com, including the Home, Products, Services, About and Contact pages. Our apps (CalendarScan, ValetGuy, Siaupoa, PassNest) have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not apply to them.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, company and project details when you use the inquiry form or email us.
  • Usage data: through Google Analytics and Google Ads cookies or similar technologies, we collect pages viewed, how you arrived (for example, which ad you clicked), device and browser type, and approximate location.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry, prepare a quote and follow up.
  • To understand how the website is used and measure advertising performance (for example, how many inquiries come from an ad).

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics and Google Ads (Google LLC): website analytics and ad conversion tracking. See how Google uses this data.
  • Formspree: delivers inquiry form submissions to our inbox.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings.
  • Install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or block and delete cookies in your browser.
  • Email us to access, correct or delete the information you have given us. California residents may exercise these rights under the CCPA/CPRA; we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the Google services listed above, and we will not discriminate against you for making a request.

Retention

Inquiry data is kept until our engagement ends or you ask us to delete it. Analytics data follows our Google Analytics retention setting (up to 14 months).

Contact and changes

Questions about this policy? Email support@goodsvalu.com. If we change this policy, we will update this page.

Effective date: November 11, 2026
