關於 Goodsvalu

帶來真正價值的技術

Goodsvalu 是一間軟體開發公司，為個人與企業打造實用的數位產品與客製化軟體解決方案。

01 · 我們是誰

讓技術把生活變得更簡單

我們相信，技術應該讓生活更輕鬆、提升效率，幫助人們用更少的複雜度完成更多事情。

我們的工作包含開發自有的軟體產品，也為有特定營運需求的企業打造專屬的應用程式。

從手機與桌面應用程式，到網站系統與 AI 工具，我們致力於結合實用的功能與直覺的設計。

我們的使命

讓技術更實用、更容易取得、更有價值。

開發能簡化日常活動、提升生產力、幫助企業更有效運作的軟體，把真實世界的挑戰轉化為有用的數位解決方案。

我們的願景

打造更好的數位體驗

我們期待一個未來：個人與企業都能使用簡單、有效率，而且能隨需求調整的軟體。透過用心的設計與持續創新，創造長久的價值。

02 · 我們的做法

從問題出發，持續改進

  1. 01
    了解問題

    先釐清每個軟體背後的挑戰、目標與實際需求。

  2. 02
    有目的地設計

    整個開發過程都重視清楚、易用與實用。

  3. 03
    用心打造

    選擇適合每個專案的技術，開發穩定、好維護的應用程式。

  4. 04
    持續改進

    軟體會不斷演進，使用者回饋、維護與改進都是產品生命週期的一部分。

03 · 我們的不同

同時做自有產品與客製開發

Goodsvalu 結合兩種互補的角度：為一般使用者開發軟體產品，以及為企業的特定需求打造客製化解決方案。

自己開發、上架並營運產品，讓我們更專注在實用的功能、有效率的流程，以及真實的使用情境。

04 · 開發原則

我們相信的事

好的軟體不需要複雜，也不應該拿使用者的資料交換便利。

簡單

每個畫面只做一件事，讓使用者不需要說明書。

隱私

能留在裝置上的資料，就不上傳。

由人確認

AI 負責找出資訊，最後的決定交給使用者。

長期維護

寫給未來的自己看得懂的程式碼與文件。

About Goodsvalu

Technology Designed to Deliver Real Value

Goodsvalu is a software development company creating practical digital products and customized software solutions for individuals and businesses.

01 · Who we are

Making life easier with technology

We believe technology should make life easier, improve efficiency, and help people accomplish more with less complexity.

Our work includes developing our own software products as well as building tailored applications for businesses with specific operational needs.

From mobile and desktop applications to web-based systems and AI-powered tools, we aim to combine useful functionality with intuitive design.

Our mission

Making Technology More Practical, Accessible, and Valuable.

To develop software that simplifies everyday activities, enhances productivity and helps businesses operate more effectively, turning real-world challenges into useful digital solutions.

Our vision

Building Better Digital Experiences

We envision a future where individuals and businesses can access software that is simple to use, efficient and adaptable. Through thoughtful design and continuous innovation, we aim to create lasting value.

02 · Our approach

From the problem to continuous improvement

  1. 01
    Understand the problem

    We start by identifying the challenges, goals and practical requirements behind each solution.

  2. 02
    Design with purpose

    We emphasize clarity, usability and functionality throughout.

  3. 03
    Build with care

    We build reliable, maintainable applications with technologies suited to each project.

  4. 04
    Improve continuously

    Software evolves. Feedback, maintenance and improvement are part of the product lifecycle.

03 · What makes us different

Products and custom development, side by side

Goodsvalu combines two complementary perspectives: developing software products for everyday users and creating custom solutions for specific business requirements.

Building, releasing and running our own products keeps us focused on practical features, efficient workflows and real-world usability.
