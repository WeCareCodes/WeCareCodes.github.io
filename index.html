06 · FAQ

常見問題

費用怎麼計算？

依功能範圍與時程報價，不採固定套餐。來信後我們會先提供概略的費用區間；規格確認後，再提供正式報價與時程。

開發大概需要多久？

依範圍而定。形象網站通常以「週」計，App 第一版通常以「月」計。概略報價時會一併提供預估時程。

原始碼和帳號歸誰？

結案後原始碼交付給你。App Store 與 Google Play 開發者帳號、網域和雲端服務，建議以你的名義申請，我們協助設定與上架。

上線之後誰負責維護？

可以另外簽訂維護合約，處理系統更新、新版 iOS／Android 相容、錯誤修正與小幅改版；也可以在需要時再個別委託。

我需要先準備什麼？

不需要完整的規格書。說明想解決的問題、主要使用者是誰，再附上一兩個參考的網站或 App，就足夠開始討論。

AI 功能會把我的資料送到哪裡？

規劃階段就會說明資料流向，能在裝置上處理的就不上傳。需要使用雲端 AI 服務時，我們會選擇承諾不以 API 資料訓練模型的服務，並寫進隱私權政策。

06 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is pricing calculated?

We quote by scope and timeline rather than fixed packages. You get a rough range first; once the spec is agreed, we give a firm quote and schedule.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A company website is usually measured in weeks, a first app version in months. The rough estimate includes a timeline.

Who owns the code and accounts?

You receive the source code at the end. App Store and Google Play developer accounts, domains and cloud services are best registered in your name; we help with setup and release.

Who maintains it after launch?

You can sign a maintenance agreement covering updates, new iOS/Android compatibility, bug fixes and small changes, or hire us case by case.

What do I need to prepare?

No full spec needed. Tell us the problem, who the users are, and one or two reference websites or apps. That’s enough to start.

Where does AI send my data?

We explain the data flow during planning and keep processing on the device where possible. When a cloud AI service is needed, we choose one that commits not to train on API data, and say so in the privacy policy.
