Privacy Policy

BookAccountTracker · Last updated: 8 September 2026

English · 繁體中文

In one sentence: your bookkeeping data never leaves your phone. The only thing that is ever sent off the device is a receipt photo or a line of text you explicitly submit for AI recognition — and only after you have agreed to it.

1. Who we are

BookAccountTracker (“the App”) is developed and operated by Goodsvalu Inc. (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question, contact support@goodsvalu.com.

2. What stays on your device

The App is local-first. All of the following is stored in a database file on your phone and is never uploaded to us:

  • Ledgers, accounts, categories and budgets
  • Every transaction — amount, date, merchant, notes, currency
  • Your app settings, including your display language and currency
  • Reminder schedules (all notifications are scheduled locally on the device; the App does not use push notifications and never registers a push token)

Backups are produced on demand and handed to your operating system’s share sheet. Where a backup file goes — your own cloud drive, email, or local storage — is entirely your choice. We never receive it.

3. What is sent off the device, and when

Only when you use AI recognition, and only after you have given consent on the in-app consent screen. There are exactly two cases:

You do thisWhat is sent
Take or pick a receipt photo and confirm The image, resized and re-encoded as JPEG on your device before it is sent
Type or dictate a description and tap submit That text

The request also carries the names of your expense categories, so the AI can pick one that already exists in your books, plus today’s date, your app version and a random one-time request identifier.

Photos

  • A photo is never written to permanent storage and is never displayed anywhere in the App.
  • Before sending, the image is resized and re-encoded. This process does not carry over the original file’s EXIF metadata, so location data is not transmitted.
  • As soon as recognition finishes — whether it succeeded or failed — the temporary file is deleted from your device.

Voice

The App has no speech-recognition component and requests no microphone permission for this purpose. When you “speak” an entry you are using your phone keyboard’s own dictation button, which is a function of your operating system. The App only ever receives the finished text. No audio is ever recorded, accessed or transmitted by the App.

4. What our server stores

Requests pass through our own server before reaching the AI provider, so that we can count usage and keep costs under control. That server keeps no content. Its database has no column capable of holding an image or any text you wrote — this is a structural guarantee, not just a promise.

5. What we never collect

  • No name and no phone number
  • No password — signing in uses a one-time code sent to your email, so there is nothing to store and nothing to leak
  • No contacts, calendar, location or photo library contents
  • No advertising identifiers; there is no advertising in the App. The App does read one device identifier provided by your operating system (Apple's vendor identifier, or Android's SSAID), used only to stop the first-month allowance being claimed over and over. It is not used for advertising or for tracking you across other apps or websites, and it is never sent to Anthropic — our own server hashes it and keeps only the hash.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of any kind is included in the App
  • No financial account credentials — the App does not connect to any bank

6. Your account

AI recognition requires an account. You sign in with an email address and a one-time code we send to it — there is no password. Everything else in the App works without an account.

The account exists for one purpose: to keep your monthly free allowance attached to you rather than to the device. Tying it to the device meant a reinstall handed out a fresh allowance, which we would ultimately have to pay for. Your email address is not used for marketing, and we do not send you anything other than sign-in codes. A credential for the session is held in your device’s secure keychain.

8. International transfer

We are established in the United States. If you are located outside the United States, submitting a photo or text for AI recognition transfers that content to the United States for processing by Anthropic PBC. You are told this on the consent screen before the first transfer, and this transfer only happens because you chose to use the feature. The usage records described in section 4 are held on servers in Japan and are accessible to us in the United States.

9. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

  • Consent — for sending photos or text to the AI provider. You give it on the consent screen and may withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy, with no effect on the rest of the App.
  • Legitimate interests — for the usage records in section 4, to enforce the free allowance, detect abuse and keep the service financially sustainable.

10. Retention

  • Photos and text: not retained by us at any point. Held in memory only for the duration of the request.
  • Usage records: kept while your account exists.
  • The first-month allowance hashes: kept even after you delete your account. That is deliberate — deleting them would defeat their only purpose. They cannot be turned back into your email address or device identifier, and they are not used for anything else.
  • Your bookkeeping data: kept on your device until you delete it. We cannot access it and cannot delete it for you.

11. Your rights and controls

  • Withdraw AI consent: Settings → Privacy → AI Recognition & Data Processing. Nothing is sent afterwards.
  • Delete your account: Settings → About & Support → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your email address and all usage records described in section 4, with one deliberate exception: the one-way hashes that stop the first-month allowance being claimed repeatedly are kept (see section 10). It does not delete the books on your phone, because we cannot reach them.
  • Delete your local data: Settings → Data → Reset, or simply uninstall the App.
  • Export your data: Settings → Data → Backup produces a complete database file; transaction lists can also be exported as CSV, Excel or PDF.
  • Access, rectification, objection: since we hold no personal content, these mostly apply to the usage records — write to support@goodsvalu.com.

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled in a way that affects you, we will update this page and, where the change concerns AI processing, ask for your consent again inside the App rather than relying on your previous agreement.

14. Contact

support@goodsvalu.com

一句話:你的帳目資料從不離開手機。唯一會送出裝置的,只有你明確 送出去做 AI 辨識的那一張收據照片或那一段文字,而且必須你先同意過。

1. 我們是誰

BookAccountTracker(以下稱「本 App」)由 Goodsvalu Inc.(以下稱「我們」)開發與營運。 隱私相關問題請聯絡 support@goodsvalu.com。

2. 留在你裝置上的資料

本 App 以本機為主。以下全部存在你手機裡的資料庫檔案,從不上傳給我們:

  • 帳本、帳戶、分類與預算
  • 每一筆交易——金額、日期、商家、備註、幣別
  • 你的 App 設定,包含顯示語言與幣別
  • 提醒排程(所有通知都在裝置本機排程;本 App 不使用推播通知,也從不註冊推播 token)

備份檔是你按下按鈕才產生,並交給作業系統的分享面板。檔案最後去哪裡——你自己的 雲端硬碟、信箱、或本機儲存空間——完全由你決定,我們不會收到。

3. 什麼情況下會送出資料

只有你使用 AI 辨識時,而且必須你已經在 App 內的同意畫面上同意過。就兩種情況:

你做的動作送出的內容
拍照或選相簿照片並確認該圖片(在你的裝置上先縮小並重新編碼成 JPEG 才送出)
輸入或口述一段描述後按送出那段文字

請求另外會帶上你的支出分類名稱(讓 AI 從你已有的分類裡挑一個)、今天的日期、 App 版本,以及一個隨機的一次性請求識別碼。

照片

  • 照片絕不寫入永久儲存空間,也絕不在 App 的任何畫面顯示。
  • 送出前會先縮小並重新編碼。這個過程不會沿用原始檔的 EXIF 中繼資料,因此 不會傳送位置資訊。
  • 辨識一結束——不論成功或失敗——暫存檔立刻從你的裝置刪除。

語音

本 App 沒有任何語音辨識元件,也不為此請求麥克風權限。你「口述」記帳時用的是手機 鍵盤自己的聽寫按鈕,那是作業系統的功能。本 App 從頭到尾只拿到聽寫完成的文字。 本 App 從不錄製、讀取或傳送任何音訊。

4. 我們的伺服器存了什麼

請求會先經過我們自己的伺服器再送到 AI 服務,以便計算用量與控制成本。那台伺服器 不保留內容:資料表裡根本沒有任何欄位能存放圖片或你寫的文字——這是結構上的保證, 不只是一句承諾。

帳號存在的唯一目的,是讓每月免費額度綁在你身上,而不是綁在裝置上——綁在 裝置上的話,重新安裝就能再領一次額度,而那筆錢最終是我們付。你的信箱位址不會用於 行銷,除了登入驗證碼之外我們不會寄任何東西給你。這次登入的憑證存放在你裝置的安全 金鑰儲存區。
