Report an AI result

If a recognition result is wrong, inappropriate or offensive, please tell us. You can report it from inside the App — Settings → About & Support → Report an AI Result — or email support@goodsvalu.com directly.

Useful things to include:

  • What you submitted (a photo of a receipt, or typed/dictated text)
  • What the App produced, and what it should have been
  • Your app version (Settings, bottom of the screen) and phone model

Please do not attach personal data you would rather not share — a description is usually enough.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. Ledgers, accounts, categories and every transaction stay in a database on the device and are never uploaded. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone: Settings → Data → Backup, and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone: install the App, then Settings → Data → Restore and pick that file.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up is required and there is no password. An anonymous account is created in the background purely to count your monthly AI allowance.

Can I use the App without the AI feature?

Yes, completely. Manual entry, reports, budgets, recurring transactions, backup and restore all work without ever sending anything off the device. If you declined the AI consent, or withdrew it, only photo and voice recognition are unavailable.

How do I withdraw my AI consent?

Settings → Privacy → AI Recognition & Data Processing → Withdraw Consent.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → About & Support → Delete Account. This deletes the anonymous account and its usage records from our servers. It does not delete the books on your phone — for that, use Settings → Data → Reset, or uninstall the App.

Why does AI recognition say I have run out?

Each month there is a free allowance of AI recognitions; the current limit is shown in the App. It resets at the start of each calendar month (UTC). Manual entry is never limited.

Is a photo of my receipt kept anywhere?

No. It is never written to permanent storage, never shown anywhere in the App, and it is deleted from the device the moment recognition finishes — whether it worked or not. Our server does not store it either.

回報 AI 辨識問題

附上這些資訊會很有幫助:

  • 你送出的是什麼(收據照片,或輸入/口述的文字)

常見問題

我的資料存在哪裡?

只存在你的手機裡。帳本、帳戶、分類與每一筆交易都留在裝置上的資料庫,不會上傳。 完整說明見隱私政策。

換手機時要怎麼搬資料?

舊手機:設定 → 資料 → 備份資料,把檔案存到新手機拿得到的地方。新手機:安裝本 App 後,設定 → 資料 → 還原備份,選擇該檔案。

需要註冊帳號嗎?

不需要註冊,也沒有密碼。背景會建立一個匿名帳號,唯一用途是計算你每月的 AI 額度。

不用 AI 功能也能使用嗎?

完全可以。手動記帳、報表、預算、循環交易、備份與還原都不需要送出任何資料到裝置外。 若你沒有同意或已撤回 AI 同意,只有拍照與語音辨識無法使用。

要怎麼撤回 AI 同意?

設定 → 隱私 → AI 辨識與資料處理 → 撤回同意。

要怎麼刪除帳號?

設定 → 關於與支援 → 刪除帳號。這會刪除我們伺服器上的匿名帳號與用量紀錄,但不會刪除 你手機裡的帳目——要刪除本機資料請用「設定 → 資料 → 重新設定」,或直接解除安裝。

為什麼 AI 辨識說我的次數用完了?

每個月有一定的免費辨識次數,實際上限以 App 內顯示為準,並於每個自然月(UTC)開始時 重置。手動記帳沒有任何次數限制。

我的收據照片會被留存嗎?

不會。照片絕不寫入永久儲存空間、絕不在 App 任何畫面顯示,辨識一結束(不論成功或 失敗)就立刻從裝置刪除。我們的伺服器也不會儲存它。
