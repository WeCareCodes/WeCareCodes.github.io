Support

ValetGuy · Goodsvalu Inc.

Email wecare@goodsvalu.com. We usually reply within two business days. Please include your organization name and, for a problem with a car, its ticket number.

Signing in

ValetGuy sends a one-time code to your email; there is no password. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check the spam folder and ask for a new code. An account is signed in on one phone at a time: signing in on another phone signs out the old one.

Plans and subscriptions

The owner subscribes in the app (Settings › Plan) and the plan covers the whole organization. To change or cancel it, use Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on the iPhone that bought it; on a new phone, tap Restore purchases on the plan page. For more than 3 sites, email us about a business contract.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings › Delete account. An owner's deletion removes the whole organization and its data; this doesn't cancel an App Store subscription.

Car owners

For your car, its photos or your information, please ask the valet company that parked it; it can also delete your information on request. See the privacy policy.
