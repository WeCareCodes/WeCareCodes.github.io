How it works

From the curb and back, in four steps.

Every car follows the same path, and everyone on the team sees where it is.

1

Check in

Scan the plate with the camera, then record make, color, mileage and condition photos — one step per page. The owner gets a digital ticket with a QR code.

2

Park

Pick the parking spot and where the key is when the car is parked. Spots and hooks in use are locked, and a spot frees up as soon as the car is brought to the curb.

3

Request

The owner opens the ticket page on their phone, sees the car's status live and requests it with one tap. The team sees it on the board right away.

4

Hand back

Check the pickup code — or the owner's name and PIN — before handing over the keys, so nobody else can claim the car.

Privacy

Car owners' data, handled with care.

The valet company decides how ValetGuy is used; we store and process the data for them and use it for nothing else. Read the privacy policy.

7 days

Photos, plate, phone number and name are deleted 7 days after the car is handed back — or right away if the car owner asks the valet company.

30 days

The rest of the ticket is deleted 30 days after handover, once it is in the backup.

0 photos

Staff phones keep no customer photos after the car leaves, and none go into device backups.

Owner only

Photos leave the app only for a disputed ticket, exported by the owner, watermarked and logged.

Questions

Common questions

Do car owners need to install the app?

No. The owner gets a digital ticket that opens as a web page on their phone. From there they follow the car's status and request it when they're ready to leave.

Which devices does ValetGuy run on?

iPhone and iPad. Each account is signed in on one device at a time; signing in on another device signs out the old one.

Can I try it before subscribing?

Yes, three ways. A new organization gets 7 days of Pro. Trial mode, on the sign-in screen, runs every step on one device with no account or internet: nothing is uploaded, so there are no staff invites or owner QR links, and at hand-over the pickup code is the last 4 digits of the ticket number. And the automatic demo plays a busy stand by itself.

How do staff sign in?

With a one-time code sent to their email — there is no password to share or forget. The owner shares an invite code once, and staff tap Join with invite code. Step-by-step guides are on the help page.

What happens if the subscription ends?

Data is kept, and cars already on site can still be requested and handed back. New cars can't be checked in until the organization subscribes again.

How do I cancel?

On the iPhone that bought the plan, go to Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Deleting an account in the app does not cancel an App Store subscription.
