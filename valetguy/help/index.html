Getting started

Owners: create the organization

  1. Open ValetGuy, enter your work email and tap Send code. Type the code from the email — there is no password.
  2. Tap Create organization and fill in the organization name, region, time zone, the first site's name and your name.
  3. You're the owner. A new organization gets a 7-day Pro trial; subscribe in Settings › Plan.

Staff: join with an invite code

  1. The owner taps Settings › Invite a staff member, picks the role and sites, and shares the 8-character code. Codes expire after 7 days.
  2. The staff member signs in with their own email, taps Join with invite code and enters the code.

Each account is signed in on one device at a time. Signing in on another phone signs out the old one.

Trial mode and the demo

On the sign-in screen, Trial mode (offline) lets you try every step on this device without an account or internet: check-in, the board, parking, retrieval, hand-over, keys and spots. You're the owner of one site with every Pro feature.

  • Nothing is uploaded or backed up. Other devices, staff invites, notifications and the car owner's QR link need an account.
  • At hand-over, the pickup code is the last 4 digits of the ticket number — the confirm page shows the hint.
  • To end the trial, sign out in Settings. Trial data isn't added to an account you sign in to later.

Watch the automatic demo plays a busy stand by itself, with no setup.

Shifts

On the Pro plan, managers and valets clock in at a site to see its board, and clock out at the end of the shift. Moving to another site needs a manager's approval. The owner can clock staff in and out, and choose acting managers. Attendance records can be exported to CSV, Excel, PDF or a text summary.

On the Basic plan there are no shifts: staff see the site they're assigned to.

Checking a car in

  1. Open the Check In tab and tap Start check-in.
  2. Go through one step per page: plate (type it or scan it), make and model, color, mileage and fuel, condition photos, and the car owner's phone (optional). Use Back and Next; Cancel parking drops the car.
  3. Tap Issue ticket. Show the QR code to the car owner, or tap Share link and send it.
  4. Tap Start next check-in for the next car. After 10 minutes without a tap, the screen goes back to the start.

The valet who checked a car in can cancel it from the card while it's still being checked in.

Parking

  1. On the Board, the card shows Start parking to the valet who checked the car in, or to the one assigned to park it. Everyone else finds the actions under ⋯.
  2. When the car is parked, tap Mark parked, choose the parking spot and where the key is — a key hook, in the car, or a place you type — and tap Confirm parked.

A spot or hook in use is locked for other cars. If a parked car has to be moved, an owner or manager can use Back to checked in and pick who parks it next; the list shows what each person on shift is doing.

Requests and retrieval

  1. The car owner taps Request my car on their ticket page. If they ask in person, use Request for owner on the board.
  2. An owner or manager taps Assign attendant and picks someone; the list shows who is parking or checking in right now.
  3. The attendant taps Start retrieval, then Car ready at the curb and says whether the key is in the car or back on a hook. The parking spot frees up for the next car.

Back to parked undoes a request, with a reason that the car owner sees on their page. If the attendant leaves before the car is ready, use Reassign retriever under ⋯. The board turns a wait red after the site's Long wait after limit (owner setting in Work sites).

Handing the car back

  1. Tap Deliver. The app first shows where the key is.
  2. On Confirm delivery, check the car owner: the 4-digit pickup code on their phone, or their name and PIN. If you check them another way, write a short note.
  3. Tap Deliver. The car owner's page shows the car as handed back.

Search on the board finds a ticket by its code, plate, name, phone number or pickup code.

Keys, spots and work sites

  • Keys and Spots tabs: add the site's key hooks and parking spots, add to the list or replace it, then tap Save.
  • Settings › Work sites (owner): rename a site, change its time zone and long-wait limit, or deactivate it. A deactivated site still counts toward the plan; staff can't clock in or check cars in there.
  • Delete site frees a place in the plan. It needs no cars on site and nobody on shift. Its tickets are removed by the usual 30-day rule, and the daily backups keep its history for a year.

Backups, disputes and privacy

  • Backups (owner, Pro): the server makes an automatic backup every day at the time you choose and keeps it for a year. The app can also save it to a folder you pick — iCloud Drive, Google Drive, OneDrive or the device.
  • Mark as disputed keeps a ticket and its photos until the dispute is closed. Only the owner can export its photos, one by one, watermarked and logged.
  • Car owners' photos, plate, phone and name are deleted 7 days after hand-over; the rest of the ticket after 30 days. The owner can erase personal data right away when a car owner asks. See the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

The sign-in code doesn't arrive.
Check the spam folder and ask for a new code; use the newest one.
"Signed in on another device"
The account was signed in on another phone. Sign in again here; the other phone is signed out.
Cars can't be checked in.
The site may be deactivated, or the plan has ended. Cars already on site can still be requested and handed back.
The car owner's code doesn't match.
Make sure it's the same ticket's page, and the car owner tapped This is my car — link this phone.
