05 · FAQ

常見問題

要做 iOS、Android，還是兩個都做？

取決於你的使用者用什麼手機。我們會在初步討論時依目標客群建議先做哪一個平台，或兩個平台同步開發。

已經有 App，可以請你們接手嗎？

可以。我們會先檢視現有的原始碼、帳號與上架狀態，評估繼續維護或部分重寫哪個比較划算，再提供報價。

上架被退件怎麼辦？

審核意見由我們協助解讀與回覆，需要修改的地方修正後重新送審。

開發者帳號要用誰的？

建議以你的公司名義申請 Apple 與 Google 開發者帳號，App 的所有權才會在你手上；我們協助申請與設定。

05 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

iOS, Android, or both?

It depends on what phones your users carry. In the first call we’ll suggest which platform to start with, or build both together.

Can you take over an existing app?

Yes. We first review the code, accounts and store status, assess whether maintaining or partly rewriting makes more sense, then quote.

What if the app is rejected in review?

We interpret and reply to review feedback, fix what’s needed and resubmit.

Whose developer accounts should we use?

We recommend Apple and Google developer accounts in your company’s name so you own the app; we help with signup and setup.
