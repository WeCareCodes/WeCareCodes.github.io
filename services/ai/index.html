05 · FAQ

常見問題

AI 會辨識錯誤嗎？

會。所以我們把流程設計成 AI 提供建議、由人確認，錯誤不會直接寫進系統；也會用你的真實資料測試辨識準確度。

我的資料會被拿去訓練 AI 嗎？

我們會選擇承諾不以 API 資料訓練模型的服務，並在規劃階段說明資料流向與保留期間。

可以先小規模試做嗎？

可以，也建議這樣做。先挑一個最耗人力的流程做概念驗證，確認效果與成本後再擴大。

AI 服務的費用怎麼算？

多數雲端 AI 依用量計費。我們會依你的預估用量試算每月成本，並在報價中分開列出。

05 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will the AI make mistakes?

Yes. That’s why AI suggests and people confirm, so errors don’t go straight into your system. We also test accuracy on your real data.

Will my data be used to train AI?

We choose services that commit not to train on API data, and explain the data flow and retention during planning.

Can we start small?

Yes, and we recommend it. Pick the most labor-intensive workflow for a proof of concept, then expand once results and costs are clear.

How are AI service costs charged?

Most cloud AI is billed by usage. We estimate the monthly cost from your expected volume and list it separately in the quote.
